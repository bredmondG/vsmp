Put your picture on the very slow movie player. It gets one {{ frame_interval_s }}-second turn on the e-paper panel, then the film carries on.

How it works

The player shows one frame of a film every {{ frame_interval_s }} seconds, {{ frames_per_hour }} frames an hour. Uploaded pictures wait in a first-in, first-out queue. When a picture's turn comes it is shown for one slot, then the film carries on where it paused. An upload never interrupts the image already on screen, and when the queue is empty the movie keeps playing.

Pictures are converted to grayscale and scaled to fit the panel, with bars added if the shape does not match.
