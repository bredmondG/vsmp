{# --- what the VSMP is ------------------------------------------------ #}

This is a very slow movie player. It shows one frame of a film on an e-paper panel every 150 seconds — 24 frames an hour. It is made to be glanced at, like a picture on the wall that barely moves.

{# --- what happens to an uploaded image ------------------------------- #}

Your picture joins a first-in, first-out queue. When its turn comes it is shown for one 150-second slot, then the film carries on where it paused — an upload never interrupts the image already on screen. Pictures are converted to grayscale and scaled to fit the panel, with white bars if the shape does not match. When the queue is empty, the movie just keeps playing.
